# Sources and rights

This independent demonstration uses public NYC TLC records and original synthetic simulations. No proprietary Uber data, credentials, private correspondence, application materials or trained model weights are included.

NYC TLC is the source of the underlying trip data. Its data documentation and terms apply; the agency does not guarantee accuracy or completeness. The small committed panel aggregates eligible trips into route-hour medians and counts.

Chronos-2 is an Amazon time-series foundation model. Upstream code and weights retain their own license and notices; see the pinned model and official `amazon-science/chronos-forecasting` repository. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Uber, Amazon or the paper authors.

The economic simulations cite Buchholz et al. (2025), Econometrica, DOI 10.3982/ECTA18838, for the qualitative importance of price and waiting-time preferences. No claim to reproduce the paper's estimates or Liftago market is made.

No separate broad license grant for the original demonstration code has been specified. Public visibility is for inspection and discussion; dependencies and third-party material retain their respective rights.
